BMW M8 Overview:

The BMW M8 is a high-performance luxury sports car produced by BMW M, the company's performance division. It serves as the top-tier model in the 8 Series lineup and is available in Coupe, Convertible, and Gran Coupe body styles.

Key Features:
- Engine: Powered by a 4.4-liter BMW M TwinPower Turbo V8 engine.
- Performance: Capable of producing up to 617 horsepower in the Competition trim, allowing for rapid acceleration (0-60 mph in approximately 3.0 seconds).
- Drivetrain: Features the M xDrive all-wheel-drive system, which can be configured for rear-wheel-drive bias for a more traditional sports car feel.
- Transmission: Equipped with an 8-speed M Steptronic automatic transmission.
- Interior: Combines luxury materials like Merino leather and carbon fiber trim with driver-focused technology, including the BMW Live Cockpit Professional and M-specific displays.
- Design: Aggressive styling with aerodynamic enhancements, a wider stance, and signature M quad exhaust pipes.

The M8 is designed to be a "gentleman's express," offering a blend of grand touring comfort and track-ready performance.
