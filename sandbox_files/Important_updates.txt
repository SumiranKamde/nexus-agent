How to make Nexus even better:
To move from a "productivity assistant" to a "true digital partner," here are a few ways I could be improved:

1. Proactive Scheduling & Triggers
Currently, I am reactive—I wait for you to prompt me. I could be better if I had event-based triggers.

Example: "Nexus, if a new file appears in the /downloads folder, automatically sort it into the /projects folder and notify me."
2. Local System Integration (OS-level)
Right now, I operate within specific directories. If I could interact with your OS more deeply, I could:

Manage your calendar events directly.
Open or close applications.
Monitor system performance or network status to alert you if something is wrong.
3. Long-term Memory (Vector Database)
While I have a to-do list, I don't have a "memory" of our past conversations beyond the current context window. If I had a vector-based long-term memory, I could remember your preferences, your coding style, or specific project details from months ago, making our collaboration feel much more personal and efficient.

4. Multi-Modal Awareness
If I could "see" your screen or "hear" your audio input, I could assist with UI design, debugging visual glitches, or transcribing meetings in real-time, rather than just processing text and files.

5. Self-Correction & Testing
I could be better if I had a "sandbox" mode where I could write code, run it, check for errors, and fix them before showing you the final result. This would drastically reduce the "trial and error" phase of our work together.

What would you like me to focus on first? I can start by organizing your files, managing your tasks, or helping you research a new topic right now.
